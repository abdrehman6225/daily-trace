    Today's paper is about finding where agent systems actually fail before you spend effort fixing them. The lead is a study that evolved whole memory programs and beat Mem0 and Zep with two rules you can test in your engine this week: keep the raw turns next to the extracted facts, and do date and count arithmetic in code. The LeWM study makes the same point for world models: a better planner, with no retraining, took long-horizon success from 22% to 85%. Anthropic's eval playbook covers how to measure gains like that honestly. On the RL side, LGPO and NOPO both question parts of GRPO-style training that most people take for granted. Cloudflare's spending caps, Gemini 4 Hydrogen's planner/executor handoff and vLLM's Inkling 2 write-up are the strongest systems and model pieces.
